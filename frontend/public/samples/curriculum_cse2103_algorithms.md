# CSE 2103: Algorithms & Complex Analysis

- **Week 1:** Review of asymptotic complexity analysis, master theorem, and recurrence relations.
- **Week 2:** Recursion and the call stack in algorithm design, divide and conquer paradigms.
- **Week 3:** Binary Search Trees (BST) review and balanced search trees (AVL rotations, balance factors).
- **Week 4:** Heaps and heap-sort: Binary Heaps, Priority Queues, decrease-key operations, and Heap-Sort algorithm.
- **Week 5:** Amortized analysis: Aggregate method, Accounting method, and Potential method.
- **Week 6:** Graph traversal algorithms: Breadth-First Search (BFS) and Depth-First Search (DFS).
- **Week 7:** Shortest Path algorithms: Dijkstra's algorithm, Bellman-Ford, and Floyd-Warshall.
- **Week 8:** Minimum Spanning Trees (MST): Prim's and Kruskal's greedy algorithms, Disjoint Set Union (DSU).
- **Week 9:** Dynamic Programming: Memoization, tabulation, subproblem optimization, 0/1 Knapsack, and LCS.
- **Week 10:** Network flow: Ford-Fulkerson method, Edmonds-Karp algorithm, and Min-Cut Max-Flow theorem.
- **Week 11:** NP-completeness: P vs NP class, polynomial-time verification, 3-SAT reduction techniques.
- **Week 12:** Approximation algorithms and randomized algorithm strategies.
