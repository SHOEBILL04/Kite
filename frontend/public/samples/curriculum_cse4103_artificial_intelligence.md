# CSE 4103: Artificial Intelligence & Knowledge Engineering

- **Week 1:** Introduction to Artificial Intelligence, Intelligent Agents, Rationality, Agent Environments (Deterministic vs Stochastic, Static vs Dynamic).
- **Week 2:** Uninformed Search Strategies: Breadth-First Search (BFS), Depth-First Search (DFS), Uniform Cost Search (Dijkstra), Iterative Deepening.
- **Week 3:** Informed (Heuristic) Search: Greedy Best-First Search, A* Search algorithm, Admissibility and Consistency of Heuristics.
- **Week 4:** Local Search & Optimization: Hill Climbing, Simulated Annealing, Genetic Algorithms, Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSP), Arc Consistency.
- **Week 5:** Adversarial Search & Game Playing: Minimax algorithm, Alpha-Beta Pruning, Evaluation Functions, Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS).
- **Week 6:** Knowledge Representation & Logic: Propositional Logic, First-Order Logic (FOL), Unification, Resolution Principle, Forward & Backward Chaining.
- **Week 7:** Reasoning under Uncertainty: Probabilistic Reasoning, Joint Probability Distributions, Bayes Rule, Independence & Conditional Independence.
- **Week 8:** Bayesian Networks: Graph structure, Conditional Probability Tables (CPTs), Exact Inference (Variable Elimination), Sampling algorithms.
- **Week 9:** Sequential Decision Making: Markov Decision Processes (MDPs), Bellman Equation, Value Iteration, Policy Iteration.
- **Week 10:** Reinforcement Learning: Q-Learning, Temporal Difference (TD) Learning, Deep Q-Networks (DQN), Exploration vs Exploitation (Epsilon-greedy).
- **Week 11:** Natural Language Processing & Knowledge Graphs: Entity Extraction, Knowledge Graphs, Vector Embeddings, Transformer architectures.
- **Week 12:** AI Ethics, Alignment & Governance: Algorithmic bias, Alignment problem, Safety guardrails, LLM evaluation metrics.
